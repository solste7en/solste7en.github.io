Privacy Policy — Atlas Whoop Sync

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Atlas Whoop Sync is a personal application created and used by a single individual (the app owner) to access that individual's own WHOOP data through the official WHOOP API.

Data accessed

The app accesses the owner's own WHOOP profile, body measurements, cycles, recovery, sleep, and workout data, solely at the owner's request.

How the data is used

The data is used only for the owner's personal health and fitness tracking and review. It is processed by the owner's personal assistant tooling on the owner's behalf.

Data sharing

No data accessed through this app is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app has no other users.

Data storage and security

API credentials and tokens are held in secure credential storage. Retrieved data is stored only in the owner's personal records.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke this app's access at any time from their WHOOP account settings, which immediately stops all further data access.

Contact

The app owner can be reached through the WHOOP account associated with this application.
